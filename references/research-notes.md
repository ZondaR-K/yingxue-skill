# Theory notes and limitations

- **Incongruity / incongruity-resolution:** humor can arise from an interpretable divergence from expectation; mere randomness typically does not suffice. This is a family of accounts, not a single universally accepted algorithm.
- **Benign Violation Theory:** a norm violation may be funny when also appraised as benign. Social distance, target sensitivity and recent harm strongly influence judgments. Warren & McGraw (2010), *Psychological Science*, https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797610376073
- **Running gag:** recurring comic device with recognizable invariant and meaningful contextual variation, especially in narrative comedy; this is a dramaturgical category, not a diagnostic test.
- **Semantic reframing:** linguistic ambiguity and contextual reinterpretation often underpin wordplay; homophones alone may be contrived.

These are creative heuristics. They cannot guarantee laughter, transfer to all audiences, or establish a measurable psychological intervention.
