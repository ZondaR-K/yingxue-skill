# Candidate critique protocol

Read proposed joke and original context. First flag violations of **facts**, **scope**, **relationship boundaries**, **forced dialect**, and **misleading attribution**. Reject or repair before ranking.

For surviving candidates, reason about:
1. Does the final turn reinterpret something planted in the setup?
2. Can an ordinary reader reconstruct the relationship without explanation?
3. Is the incongruity surprising but traceable, rather than random?
4. Are there filler phrases, clichés, or unnecessary meta-explanation?
5. Is the joke appropriate given audience, target, emotion and stakes?
6. Does a recurring character stay internally consistent while changing the setting?

Revise once; prefer compact naturally spoken Chinese. Output scores only upon user request, and label them as editorial judgment, not objective funniness.
